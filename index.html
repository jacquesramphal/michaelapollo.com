About Michael

A career helping leaders and teams develop the dynamics for strategic execution.

Michael Apollo is an executive advisor, speaker, podcast host, and founder with more than 15 years of experience and advising leaders, teams, and organizations.

Because of his work with 450+ corporate leadership teams and 100,000 professionals across industries, his organizations have been selected as vendors for the World Health Organization, the Canadian Prime Minister’s Office and Fortune 500 organizations. His work has been featured in leading psychological journals and recognized as “Best in Practice” by the Association for Talent Development and has supported leaders across financial services, technology, healthcare, and all levels of government.
